ABOUT US

Mission

To provide potable water to the municipality of Taft as a partner in promoting economic growth, environmental stewardship, and protection of health and well-being of its residents.

Vision

That the Taft Water District, with sufficient potable water, be the most reliable and economically viable service provider in meeting the needs of a fully developed Taft municipality while ensuring the preservation of our natural resources.
